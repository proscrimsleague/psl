BSPC Season 1 — PC Grand Final

Final Results · 20 Teams

NFAM Medellín won the PC Grand Final with 232 points, 46 more than runner-up Ascend Hydra (186). Mafia Syndicate took 3rd with 181. The top fragger was Kai (NFAM Medellín) with 63 kills.

  1. 20 teamsThe best 10 teams from Group A and the best 10 from Group B played the Grand Final.
  2. 4 matchesTeams play several matches. M1 to M4 in the table are the points a team earned in each one.
  3. Add it upTotal points = M1 + M2 + M3 + M4. The team with the highest total wins.
  4. KillsA kill is one eliminated opponent. The player tables show kills per match and in total.

About PSL

Pro Scrims League is a competitive Blood Strike ecosystem focused on organized scrims, tournaments, rankings, teams, players and community development.

PSL aims to create a structured environment where competitive players and organizations can consistently compete, develop and grow — from first scrim to championship stage.
