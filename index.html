About PSL

Pro Scrims League is a competitive Blood Strike ecosystem focused on organized scrims, tournaments, rankings, teams, players and community development.

PSL aims to create a structured environment where competitive players and organizations can consistently compete, develop and grow — from first scrim to championship stage.
