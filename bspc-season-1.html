How BSPC Season 01 Worked

New to the tournament? This is the whole format in four steps.

  1. 2 platformsPC and Mobile ran as two separate tournaments. Each platform had its own champion and its own prize.
  2. 2 groups eachEvery platform had a Group A day and a Group B day. Teams were ranked by the points they earned.
  3. Top 10 advanceThe top 10 teams from Group A and the top 10 from Group B went through. That makes 20 teams per Grand Final.
  4. Grand FinalFour matches. Points add up, and the team with the highest total wins 10,000 gold.
